# Cherri Hosting — Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-19  
**Applies to:** All users of the Cherri Hosting platform

---

## 1. What Cherri Hosting is for

Cherri Hosting lets you publish static websites and web apps to IPFS using the Pi Network ecosystem.
Every deployment is tied to a real Pi Network identity — you are accountable for what you publish,
and so are we. That accountability is what keeps this platform safe for everyone in the Pi community.

---

## 2. What you must never publish

By deploying on Cherri Hosting, you confirm that your content does **not**:

1. **Impersonate brands, wallets, or Pi Network.** No phishing pages, fake login screens,
   or pages designed to trick users into surrendering credentials, private keys, or Pi coins.

2. **Target Pi users with scams or fraud.** No fake investment schemes, wallet drainers,
   rug-pull sites, Ponzi schemes, or any scheme to steal Pi or other assets.

3. **Distribute malware, ransomware, or spyware.** No files or scripts designed to harm
   the devices or data of your visitors.

4. **Serve illegal content.** No content that is illegal in your jurisdiction or in the
   jurisdiction of your users — including copyright-infringing material, CSAM, or content
   that facilitates serious crimes.

5. **Host crypto wallet drainers or attack scripts.** No JavaScript that drains wallets,
   mines cryptocurrency on visitors' devices, or launches attacks against other systems.

6. **Violate privacy rights.** No unauthorized collection or exposure of personal data.

---

## 3. How Cherri enforces this

- **Pre-publish scan:** Every build output is scanned for known malware signatures, phishing
  indicators, obfuscated JavaScript, and wallet-drainer patterns before being pinned to IPFS.
- **Operator review:** Suspicious deployments are held for human review before going live.
- **Takedown:** We can unpin any deployment and remove domain mappings at any time.
- **Identity accountability:** Violations are tied to your Pi Network identity. Abuse results
  in your Pi identity being banned from the platform, and all your deployments taken down.

---

## 4. Your attestation

When you deploy on Cherri Hosting, you attest that:

1. You have read this policy.
2. The content you are publishing complies with every requirement in Section 2.
3. You are legally permitted to publish this content.
4. You understand that violations result in immediate takedown and a platform ban.

**By clicking "I agree" during deployment, you are making this attestation.**
We record the date, time, and policy version with every deployment.

---

## 5. Contact

If you believe a Cherri Hosting site violates this policy, contact the operator via
the in-app support channel.

---

*This policy may be updated. You will be asked to re-attest when the version changes.*
